I'm a Computer Science student at Georgia State University working at the intersection of **research, data, and AI/ML**, with most of my work aimed at the energy transition. As an undergraduate researcher in GSU's DICE Lab, I study urban energy systems through geospatial data, and I bring that research mindset to everything I build: start from a real question, assemble a clean dataset, and test the answer carefully.

On the data and machine learning side, I forecast day-ahead electricity prices, optimize battery dispatch, detect anomalies in cloud spend, and design agentic pipelines that validate supplier emissions data. I build this work the way software engineers do, with modular Python, version control in **Git**, and deployment on **Azure**, so it holds up well beyond a notebook.

### Selected work

| Project | Challenge | Outcome |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **[CarbonTrace](https://github.com/lithikajmalothu-16/carbontrace)** | Supplier emissions data arrives incomplete and inconsistent | Agentic Azure pipeline that validates Scope 3 data and prepares it for audit |
| **[Battery Arbitrage Optimizer](https://github.com/lithikajmalothu-16/battery-arbitrage-optimizer)** | Deciding when a battery should charge and discharge | Day-ahead price forecast plus an optimized schedule: **66.5% capture ratio** |
| **[FinOps Cost Allocation](https://github.com/lithikajmalothu-16/finops-cost-allocation)** | Shared Azure resources hide who is spending what | Usage-based allocation and four KQL detectors: **9 of 9 anomalies caught** |
| **[Power BI Sales Analysis](https://github.com/lithikajmalothu-16/choco-analysis-powerbi)** | Raw spreadsheet data with no reporting layer | Star schema, DAX measures, and an executive dashboard |
